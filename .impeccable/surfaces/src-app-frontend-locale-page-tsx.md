## Scope

The Tensoract company site: home, about, products index and detail, blog, contact.
Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

## Audience and job

Investors judging whether this team executes, and enterprises judging whether the
software can run inside their operation. Both arrive cold, give the page under a
minute, and are deciding one thing: is this seven-person company in Thủ Đức real?

## Action

Email, directly. One address, stated plainly, repeated at the close. No booking
flow, no gated PDF, no lead form as the primary path. The contact form stays as a
secondary route only.

## Proof available

Four products actually running; named infrastructure partners; Startup Wheel 2024
entry; founder on record; 2022 registration. Nothing else is verified — no
metrics, customers, testimonials, or photography. The design must be strong with
type, space and authored illustration alone, and must not leave holes shaped like
missing logos.

## Direction

**Giấy dó & mộc bản** — Vietnamese handmade paper and woodblock print. Warm
fibrous paper ground, warm black ink, cinnabar red used only where something is
attested. Hand-cut illustrative forms with slight ink bleed carry the office,
team and product imagery, authored rather than photographed and honest about it.

Chosen because the audience is judging credibility and patience, and because the
previous industrial-label world read as cold. Honours the user's pinned warm and
airy brief without landing on flat cream-and-serif.

## Memorable moment

The cinnabar seal. It appears only against things an outside party attested — the
competition entry, the registration facts — pressed slightly off-square with real
ink texture, never as decoration.

## Constraints

- Never state a funding posture, in either language.
- Speak to partners, not to online sellers.
- Contact email is an unverified placeholder and must be marked as such.
- Bilingual VI/EN, both first-class; every string stays CMS-editable.

## Unresolved

Real contact email. The fifth product. Whether real photography will replace the
authored illustration.
